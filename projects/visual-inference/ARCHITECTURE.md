# Module Purpose & Boundaries

Owns open-world SAM pseudo-label discovery, edge-targeted MobileNetV4 student object detection across Horizontal Bounding Box (HBB) and Oriented Quadrilateral (Quad) geometries, and end-to-end OWLv2- and SigLIP-distilled open-vocabulary semantic classification without a runtime text encoder.

# Technical Contracts & Interfaces

- **Input & Canvas**: FP32 RGB `[B, 3, H, W]` letterboxed to `384x384` (`divisible by 32`), with `valid_mask` `[H, W]` and `transform = (scale, off_x, off_y, horizontal_flip)`.
- **Backbone & Bidirectional Neck**:
  - `MobileNetV4Backbone` (`mobilenetv4_conv_medium.e500_r256_in1k`) emits `(C2: 48ch, C3: 80ch, C4: 160ch, C5: 256ch)` at strides `s in (4, 8, 16, 32)`.
  - `P2LitePAN` (`neck_type="pan"`, `fpn_channels=128`) performs top-down + bottom-up depthwise-separable fusion returning `[B, 128, H/s, W/s]`; legacy `P2LiteFPN` (`96ch`) remains loadable.
- **Dual-Head Proposal Detectors (`dual_head=True`, `use_3dmf=True`)**:
  - `StudentDetector` (HBB) predicts `objectness` `[B, 1, H_l, W_l]`, FCOS `ltrb` `[B, 4, H_l, W_l]`, and `centerness` `[B, 1, H_l, W_l]`.
  - `QuadProposalDetector` (Quad) predicts `quality` `[B, 1, H_l, W_l]` and corner offsets `[B, 8, H_l, W_l]`.
  - Primary head embeds `CrossScale3DMaxFilter` (3DMF across adjacent scales + 3x3 spatial window); inference defaults `forward_eval_head(features, aux_weight=0.0)`.
- **Open-Vocabulary Heads (`student_detector/open_vocab_heads.py`)**:
  - **Option A (`DenseAnchorSemanticHead`)**: 2-layer depthwise-separable conv tower on `P2LitePAN` + 1x1 projection to L2-normalized `[N, 768]` via `forward_indexed` or static `forward_dense` (zero RoI ops).
  - **Option B (`RoIPooledSemanticHead`)**: coordinate-free dual-scale `MultiScaleRoIAlign` (`focal_margin=0.15`, `context_margin=0.45`, `output_size=5`) + LayerNorm/GELU MLP to L2-normalized `[N, 768]`.
  - **Calibration (`_ContrastiveClassifierMixin`)**: Supports `teacher_space="siglip"` (visual-centroid offset `torch.where(raw <= 0.05, 0.85 * raw.clamp(max=0.016), raw)`, asymmetric median subtraction, `2.0x` distractor margin veto `+0.004`) and `teacher_space="owlv2"` (`init_scale=20.0`, `init_bias=-6.5`, no centroid/median shift, `1.5x` distractor margin veto `+0.02`), scoring against frozen or adapted `[C, 768]` `VocabularyBank` embeddings (`sqrt(s_obj * p_cls)`).
- **Losses & Target Assignment**:
  - `TaskAlignedAssigner` / `QuadTaskAlignedAssigner`: primary 1-to-1 greedy matching (`max_one_to_one_interior_area_frac=0.90` default, `0.20` in OWLv2 HBB `TargetBuilder` and Quad `QuadTargetBuilder` so capped `building`/`tree` boxes do not create large negative dead zones) + auxiliary 1-to-many TAL (`top_k=10` HBB / `8` OWLv2 Quad / `13` legacy) and slender-aware gating; `ProposalLoss` and `QuadProposalLoss` (`use_proxy_quality_floor=False`) use native raw IoU quality targets across all box scales (the discontinuous `<16px sqrt(IoU)` quality boost was removed).
  - `OpenVocabularySigmoidLoss`: supports `"siglip"` (cosine + KL over `[37; 7]` + subspace/margin/InfoNCE + compatible GT) and `"owlv2"` (native OWLv2 768-D distillation `min_side>=10px & gt_iou>=0.40` across 37+7 axes + Core-27 closed-set supervised focal + hard-negative margin over pruned 27 classes with `street_light->pole` merge).
- **Decoders & Semantic Filtering**: `InferenceDecoder` and `QuadInferenceDecoder` share small-box objectness exponentiation (`small_box_min_side=16.0`, `small_box_score_power`, e.g., `1.25` after raw-IoU calibration vs `1.5` prior, ranking Quad candidates by clamped envelope while preserving pre-clamp `quad_validity`). `class_agnostic_nms` and `polygon_nms` (`containment_aware`, `pair_ratios > 0.15` when `class_ids=None`) feed `filter_semantic_detections`, which combines HBB + Quad overlap (`torch.maximum(q_iou, 0.85 * b_iou)`), drops `< 4.5px` micro-specks unless `p >= 0.45`, runs Pass-0 objectness-aware container suppression preserving `VALID_CONTAINER_PARTS`, and skips backdrop suppression via `BACKDROP_NO_SUPPRESS_CONTAINERS = {"building", "tree", "fence", "canopy"}`.
- **Core-27 Inference Preset (`student_detector/ontology.py`, `VocabularyBank.subset`)**: `CORE_27_CATEGORIES` collapses `street_light -> pole` (`CLOSED_MERGE_MAP`) and prunes 9 noisy/empty scarce classes (`PRUNED_SCARCE_CATEGORIES`) at deployment time via `VocabularyBank.subset(...)`, while keeping full 37-class adapted/frozen banks and arbitrary unseen open-vocabulary prompts usable on the same checkpoint.
- **Annotation Hygiene (`student_detector/annotation_hygiene.py`)**: Non-destructive in-memory filtering across `HumanGTHBBDataset`, `HumanGTQuadDataset`, `semantic_evaluation_data`, dashboard GT overlays, and SAM discovery masking (`sanitize_annotations=True`): deduplicates `nuimages`/`coco_2017` `source_hbb` vs `fitted_quad` 1-to-1 doubles (`IoU>=0.50`), collapses occluded multi-fragment `fitted_quad` sub-pieces (`IoU>=0.30` or `IoMin>=0.70`) back into their enclosing `source_hbb`, demotes noisy/nested/`<16px` (`<32px` pole major) `woodscape_rgb_fisheye` mask fragments to `ignore_region=1`, and preserves raw positive `gt_indices` cache offsets.
- **OWLv2-Large Unified Teacher Cache (`student_detector/owlv2_teacher.py`, `scripts/build_owlv2_teacher_cache.py`, `scripts/patch_owlv2_cache_bollards_pillars.py`)**: Scaled leak-free 30k WAL SQLite cache (`artifacts/cache/owlv2_teacher_30k.sqlite`, seeded from 12k via `--seed-from-db`, `457,554` centroid target embeddings, `73.01%` Top-1 / `89.17%` Top-3 over `274,299` matched Human GT; prior 12k cache at `artifacts/cache/owlv2_teacher_12k.sqlite`). Targeted patch cache `artifacts/cache/owlv2_teacher_30k_patched.sqlite` appends bollard (`pole`) & architectural column (`pillar`) OWLv2 detections across 10k images (`nuimages` 6k `+4,164 pole` / `+3,362 pillar`; `woodscape_rgb_fisheye` 4k `+0 pole` / `+456 pillar`, `0` NaNs, `0/236` train-dashboard overlap). Native 768-D OWLv2 `VocabularyBank` files live at `artifacts/sota_open_vocab/owlv2_vocabulary_bank_*.pt` (including `core27` / `31classes_core27_zeroshot`).

# Local Constraints & Gotchas

- **Mandatory FP32 Geometry & Loss under AMP**: Canvas `384^2 = 147,456 > 65,504` (FP16 max); promote quad geometry, `_quality_focal_loss`, `OpenVocabularySigmoidLoss` logits, and `train_proposal_decoder` to FP32.
- **Quad Validity Before Border Clamping**: Filter `quad_validity` on unclamped vertices prior to `.clamp(0, W)`.
- **Coordinate-Free Semantic Heads & BatchNorm Integrity**: Never feed normalized box center coords `[xc/W, yc/H]` into semantic heads; restrict WiSE-FT interpolation to conv/linear or semantic heads so BatchNorm stats stay exact.
- **Flip & Padding Invariants**: Pass `horizontal_flip` to `canvas_box_to_native`; slice `category_names` alongside `retained` geometric masks; apply `point_validity_from_pixel_mask(sample.valid_mask, ...)` in decoders so letterbox padding anchors are masked to `-inf`.
- **Synthetic vs. Empirical Centroid Bias**: Keep the `raw <= 0.05` guard inside `compute_prompt_bias` so empirical SigLIP centroids (`<= 0.02`) are damped (`0.85 * raw.clamp(max=0.016)`) while high-dot synthetic test centroids (`0.45`) still subtract in full.
- **Dashboard Visibility Gating**: `_build_sota_preds` caps rendered predictions at `<= 30/image` using joint score `sqrt(s_obj * p_cls)` after `filter_semantic_detections`.
